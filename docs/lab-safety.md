# Lab Safety Guidelines

## Isolation Rules
- Use dedicated local VMs or containers for experiments.
- Keep lab networks segmented from production and personal devices.
- Snapshot systems before risky changes.

## Authorization Rules
- Perform scans and testing only on systems you own or are authorized to assess.
- Keep written authorization for class/lab engagements.

## Privacy and Data Handling
- Never upload real packet captures with personal or sensitive data.
- Sanitize usernames, IP addresses, hostnames, and domains before sharing.
- Store forensic artifacts with integrity hashes and controlled access.

## Packet Capture and Evidence Handling
- Capture only minimal traffic needed for learning objectives.
- Document tool versions, timezone, and timeline assumptions.
- Verify hashes before/after transferring evidence files.
- Maintain a simple chain-of-custody log for case studies.
